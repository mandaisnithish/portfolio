## Platform

web

## Users

People deciding whether to interview Nithish Kaliannan Ananth for a UK graduate role. Two equal audiences:

- **Recruiters**, usually on a phone, arriving from LinkedIn or an application and skimming in under a minute to decide whether to pass the profile on.
- **Hiring managers and technical reviewers**, usually on desktop, reading the case study properly before or after an interview, and checking whether the claims hold up.

## Product Purpose

A single-page personal portfolio that gets the owner an interview. Success is a visitor emailing or messaging on LinkedIn, or forwarding the link, after understanding in seconds who this is and seeing evidence they believe.

## Positioning

A data scientist who has also worked on the instrument side: a BSc in Data Science (First Class, Coventry, 2022–2026), followed by an engineering internship building test and measurement software and learning the hardware behind the readings. The claim no generic data-science graduate can copy: they start with the decision, not the data, and distrust a clean number because they've seen how a reading gets made.

Target roles are kept deliberately broad: data science, ML engineering, analytics and software engineering, with none ranked above the others.

## Operating Context

Visitors open the link from LinkedIn, a CV or a job application. Recruiters compare many candidates quickly. Technical reviewers look for over-claiming, so every figure must be traceable to the written account.

## Product Principles

1. Every number on the page must match the written account; nothing decorative is presented as data.
2. Say it once: one full telling of each result, not repeated restatements.
3. Lead with the implication, then the evidence.
4. Name the gaps honestly (MLOps and deployment).
5. Works as a 30-second phone skim and as a 10-minute desktop read.
