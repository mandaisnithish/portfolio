## Direction contract
THESIS: The page is one AI accelerator chip explored in 3D: the lid lifts, the camera descends onto the die, and scrolling flies over floorplan blocks that are the sections. Refuses the flat dark portfolio of stacked cards and the hero-over-grid template.
OWN-WORLD: Graphite black ground (user-pinned). Blue-grey silicon blocks in distinct metal tones, copper interconnect, one copper highlight for the active block, gold-free. Square edges, hairline borders, mono for labels and readings, a readable sans for prose on solid graphite panels beside the scene.
STORY: The visitor sees a precise object with a name etched on it and four test-pad readings, flies through the blocks reading each section, and lands on the I/O ring to make contact.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left/centre: 3D chip package, lid lifting on load, camera settling over the die with the name etched in its corner. Right: name, role, statement, then four test-pad readouts (−15% MAE, −8% cost, 61% of missed SLAs on 3 routes, 5 h/week). Bond-pad nav in the top bar with a copper Contact.
FORM: AI Chip Floorplan, user-chosen from re-roll 1 (was IMPECCABLE'S PICK), extended by the user to a full 3D experience; seed:4663f14631ecb67d. Motion grammar: one camera path driven by scroll, one lid lift; reduced motion shows a still chip; no WebGL shows a flat floorplan drawing.
